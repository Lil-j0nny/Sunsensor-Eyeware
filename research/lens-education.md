# General photochromatic lens explanation

The storefront's explanation that photochromatic lenses typically become darker in sunlight and clearer indoors is general lens education, not a verified performance guarantee for a particular SunSensor lens. Warby Parker's educational guide explains that photochromic and photochromatic are terms for the same lens category and that these lenses darken in sunlight's UV light and clear indoors. [1]

No timing, UV percentage, driving behavior, coating, exact tint or branded lens technology from that guide is applied to SunSensor's listings. Those exact specifications require confirmation from SunSensor for the chosen lens.
