# Sunsensor Eyewear Opticians — verified shop facts

**Research checked:** 7 November 2026. **Scope:** only the Nairobi business represented by the official Facebook page `facebook.com/sunsensoreyewear`, TikTok account `@sunsensoreyewear`, and Instagram account `@sunsensor_eyewear_opticals`. Generic SunSensors lens products and other optical retailers were excluded.

## Verified business identity, address, and phone

- The official Facebook page is named **Sunsensor Eyewear Opticians**. Its intro says: “At Sunsensor Eyewear, we dispense prescription lenses in the lattest [sic] designer frame to ensure sytle [sic] and comfort.” The page lists **City Estate, Kenya** and **+254 720 307081**. [1]
- Multiple posts from the exact official TikTok account give the more specific shop directions: **Stewart Building, ground floor, Room S6, along Tom Mboya Street near the Ronald Ngala junction, opposite Kings Collection, Nairobi**. They repeat **0720 307081**. [2] [10]
- The same Stewart Building / Room S6 directions and phone number appear in an official Instagram post. [7]
- Because the Facebook page says “City Estate” while the repeated post directions say Stewart Building / Tom Mboya Street, use the detailed Stewart Building directions as the published shop directions but verify the location before launch. Do not add a different building or branch from search snippets alone.

## Officially published products, services, and lens claims

- The business publicly describes itself as dispensing prescription lenses in designer frames. [1]
- An official Facebook post says it offers **progressive and bifocal lenses** for near-distance reading / presbyopia. The post does not give a price. [6]
- An official Facebook post says **photochromatic anti-glare lenses cost KSh 2,000** and offers a **free eye check-up on each purchase**. The same post explicitly says prices shown on the frames are **exclusive of lenses**. This is a dated post from 6 February 2020, so it is not a current price guarantee. [4]
- An official Instagram post describes **“Acetate Optical frames” at KSh 3,000 each** (17 August 2026). [8]
- An official TikTok video from the exact account is captioned **“100% acetate.”** It does not identify the model, dimensions, prescription suitability, or stock quantity, so this should be treated as a material claim for that video’s featured item(s), not a claim that every frame is acetate. [9]
- A directly fetched official TikTok post dated 24 June 2026 lists prescription lens services for **shortsightedness, longsightedness, astigmatism, blue-block, anti-glare, photochromatic, progressive, and bifocal** lenses; it says cost depends on the prescription. The same caption says the shop provides a **free eye check-up when dispensing lenses**, transfers lenses from a broken frame to a new one, and does not accept SHIF or other insurance covers. Treat those offers and statements as dated and confirm that they remain current. [13]

- The posts use general availability wording such as “available at” and show different frame-price posts, but they do not provide per-model stock counts or a reliable live inventory. Do not label a particular color, style, size, or material as in stock unless the shop confirms it.

## Shipping, returns, and refunds

- **Shipping/delivery is not verified from the fetched page text.** Search-result snippets associated with the exact official TikTok/Instagram accounts mention countrywide delivery, but the fetched pages did not expose enough of that text to verify coverage, fees, timing, payment conditions, or whether it is still current. Treat countrywide delivery as **unverified** until the shop confirms it directly. [10] [11]
- **No official returns, exchanges, refunds, remake/warranty, or prescription-error policy was found** on the fetched Facebook, TikTok, or Instagram pages. Do not invent one for the website.
- The reference site’s `hello@sunsensoreyewear.co.ke` was identified by the parent as a placeholder email, and the domain did not resolve during the parent’s inspection. It is **not verified as an official contact** and should not be presented as the shop’s email without direct confirmation.

## Safe website-use notes

Use the exact business name, official social links, phone number, and Stewart Building directions above. Product cards should be framed as examples or dated social-post prices, not guaranteed current stock. Leave size, live availability, shipping terms, and returns/refunds as “contact shop to confirm” until the business supplies them.
